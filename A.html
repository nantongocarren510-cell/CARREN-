Livestock Rearing

Livestock rearing is the practice of keeping and caring for animals such as cattle,goat, sheep and poultry for food and income.

Types of livestock

  • Cattle
  • Goats
  • Sheep
  • Chickens

Benefits of livestock rearing

  1. Provides food such as milk,meat and eggs.
  2. Creates employment opportunities.
  3. Provides income to farmers.
